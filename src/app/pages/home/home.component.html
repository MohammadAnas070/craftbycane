Our story

Cane has been woven this way for generations.

We work with skilled artisans who split, soak and weave natural cane by hand, then turn it into furniture and decor that suits modern homes.

Each piece begins as raw cane and ends with a finish chosen for its room. Traditional weaving patterns sit alongside simple, contemporary shapes, and every product can be adapted in size and colour.

  • Natural

    cane, rope, seasoned wood

  • Handmade

    woven and finished by artisans

  • Custom

    sizes and finishes on request
